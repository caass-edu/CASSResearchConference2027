About the Conference

The Central Asian pre-university academic environment is marked by an insufficient emphasis on social sciences and humanities, as opposed to natural and technical sciences. Moreover, high school authors of accredited mentored research lack the institutional support that allows for interaction with the academia at reputable institutions across Central Asia. CASSConf2027 is an annual conference that aims to bridge this gap and foster international intellectual dialogue in an increasingly changeable geopolitical environment.

In addition to keynote speakers from Almaty Management University, the conference is honoured to welcome guest speakers from the Higher School of Economics, Moscow.

When and Where

When: 12 January, 2027.

Where: as CASSConf2027 is a hybrid conference, the event will take place at two venues simultaneously: Almaty Management University, Almaty, Kazakhstan (in-person), Zoom conference (online).

Eligibility and Scope

Eligibility: the conference is open to high school students (10–12 grades, 16–18 years of age) and undergraduate university students (first and second years). Co-authored work is not eligible for presentation.

Scope: the conference accepts international abstract submissions from across Central Asia (Kazakhstan, Uzbekistan, Turkmenistan, Tajikistan, Kyrgyzstan, Mongolia) and beyond.

Submission Requirements

We welcome applicants to share an abstract of their original work (300–350 words). The language of submission and presentation is English. The judging committee is devoted to an anonymous double-blind peer review of the abstracts.

Selected delegates will be invited to present their research in-person, at Almaty Management University, or online, via a Zoom conference. There will be 2–5 presenters per conference section. The abstracts will be published in the conference proceedings and a special issue at Almaty Management University.
